A button that looks pressable. A handle that says "pull". A ledge that looks
climbable. You can tell what to do before anyone tells you.

That's an **affordance**: the way a thing looks invites the right action.

* * *

Affordances make things feel easy. When something looks one way but works
another (a flat word that is secretly a button), people get lost, and they
blame themselves.

* * *

## Words to know

- **Affordance:** a look that invites the right action.
- **Signifier:** the specific clue that shows the affordance (the handle, the shadow).
- **False affordance:** looks usable but isn't.

* * *

## Under the hood

In screens: real buttons look like buttons, and 44-pixel touch targets
(Worlds' rule). In games: a consistent look for things you can interact with.
