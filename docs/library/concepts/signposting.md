A faint crack in a wall. A light left on. A path of worn grass. You didn't
write "go here", but the world said it anyway.

That's **signposting**: small clues that guide attention without an arrow
or a sign.

* * *

Good signposting keeps people exploring on their own, and feeling clever
when they notice. Too little, and nobody finds the secret. Too much, and it
stops being a secret.

* * *

## Words to know

- **Signposting:** hints that guide without instructions.
- **Breadcrumbs:** a trail of small clues leading somewhere.
- **Weenie:** a big landmark that pulls you toward it (a theme-park word).

* * *

## Under the hood

Often just consistent art and lighting choices; see *Visual language*. When
testing, watch where people's eyes and feet go first.
