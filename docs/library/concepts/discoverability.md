You tested it and nobody found the hidden door. The secret may be working a
little too well.

That's **discoverability**: how easily people can find that something is
there.

* * *

Secrets are meant to be a bit hidden, but the main features shouldn't be.
When people can't find something, it usually isn't their fault: the world
didn't hint enough (see *Signposting*).

* * *

## Words to know

- **Discoverability:** how findable something is.
- **Playtest:** watching real people use it, quietly.
- **Friction:** anything that makes something harder than it needs to be.

* * *

## Under the hood

The fix is usually a stronger signpost or a clearer affordance, not a
tutorial. Watch where people get stuck, then adjust the world.
