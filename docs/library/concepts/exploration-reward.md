You put treasure behind the hidden door. Now looking around is worth it.

That's an **exploration reward**: curiosity pays off.

* * *

The reward doesn't have to be big. A bit of story, a pretty view or a
funny note works too. What matters is that looking was worth it, so people
keep looking.

* * *

## Words to know

- **Exploration reward:** a payoff for going off the main path.
- **Secret:** a reward that's deliberately hard to find.
- **Critical path:** the route you must take to finish; rewards usually sit off it.

* * *

## Under the hood

Often paired with *gating* and *signposting*: a hint, a gate, then the
reward.
