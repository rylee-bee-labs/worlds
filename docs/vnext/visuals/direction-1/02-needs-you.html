If you would rather not leave

Asking Worlds to carry your answer changes nothing about who decides. The path is always the same: you ask, you approve exactly what Worlds is allowed to send, that approval is spent once, and Worlds reports back sent, failed or unknown. Asking again is a new ask, never a silent retry.

Fernway keeps the record. Worlds keeps a note that it carried it.

How to read this shore

Services sit in one of three places. Where a service sits is a claim about how close it is to needing you — it is not a score, and it is not a ranking of how important the service is.

  • Healthy answered recently
  • Needs attention a person has to decide something
  • Stale heard from, but too old to trust
  • Unknown never heard from

MOCKUP — not a prototype, and not approved. Every service name, owning system and address on this page is invented for the mockup. Nothing here was fetched from a provider, and no value shown is a real result. Outbound links point at reserved .invalid addresses, which cannot resolve.

Only Home is drawn in this direction. Connect, Memory and Settings are named so the landmarks stay honest, and are not mocked up here.
