Stale is not unknown, and neither one is an outage

Stale means Worlds heard from the service and the answer has aged out. It still has something to show you, clearly marked as old. Unknown means Worlds has never heard anything, so it has nothing to show you and says so. Both are honest answers, and both are visibly worse than a service that simply said it was fine.

Only the owning system knows whether it is broken. Worlds shows the way to get there, and says plainly that it does not know.

How to read this shore

Services sit in one of three places. Where a service sits is a claim about how close it is to needing you — it is not a score, and it is not a ranking of how important the service is.

  • Healthy answered recently
  • Needs attention a person has to decide something
  • Stale heard from, but too old to trust
  • Unknown never heard from
