Set up Worlds

Step 1 of 6: First light

Welcome to your World.

Worlds is one calm place for everything you’re building and everything you want to keep: your projects, your notes, and the rooms you connect. It runs on your server, and everything here belongs to you. Services you connect later receive only the requests you choose to make.

  1. Worlds gets itself ready while you watch.
  2. You choose how to sign in.
  3. You make it comfortable: colours, text, motion, crew.
  4. You pick a companion, or not.
  5. The door opens.

You don’t need any technical knowledge. If something goes wrong, this page says what happened and what to try.

About five minutes. You can change everything later.
